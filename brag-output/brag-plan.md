# Brag Plan: AA Fencing LLC

## What is this app?
A launch site for AA Fencing LLC — a Rochester, NY union fence contractor operating since 1992 (with Scott's Landscape & Fence). Commercial security fencing, bollards, signage, residential wood/vinyl/chain link/ornamental aluminum, plus concrete and landscaping. The site's voice is blunt and proud: "We don't cut corners, and we don't dump dry concrete mix into dry dirt holes."

## The angle
30+ years of Rochester fence work, announced like a trailer. This is not a cute joke build — it's a real veteran contractor with real jobs in real yards. The premise: let the actual work carry it. Cold-open on the brand mark, then a fast run of real installation photos, the before/after transformation, the union credentials, and the proof (4.25★ / 53 reviews) — cut to a hard "Est. 1992" close. Specific because every frame is a real AA Fencing job and the copy is theirs, not stock.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Black screen. Green line draws across (the #1f8b2e brand accent, like a fence rail snapping into place). "ROCHESTER'S TRUSTED FENCE COMPANY" slams in over a darkened real fence photo. Green pulse dot. Then the AA logo settles. Confident, not busy.

## Key moments (the middle)
- Rapid montage of 3-4 real gallery fence installs, each with a one-word/short label (VINYL PRIVACY · CHAIN LINK · ORNAMENTAL ALUMINUM · COMMERCIAL SECURITY), Ken-Burns push on each photo.
- The before/after transformation: bare yard wipes to finished fence with a green divider sweeping across (recreating the site's before/after slider as an automated reveal).
- Credentials strip: "UNION CONTRACTOR · ELITE FENCE PRODUCTS AUTHORIZED DEALER · 36" FROST-LINE POSTS · REAL WET CONCRETE" — the ticker line, delivered as punchy stacked reveals.
- Proof card: 4.25 ★★★★★ / 53 reviews, one short real quote ("Even better than we hoped!" — Rebecca B., Chili NY).

## Outro / punchline
Cut to black. Green rail draws again. "EST. 1992 · ROCHESTER, NY" then logo + "aafencing-llc.com". Optional tag line under: "Speak directly with the owners." Hard, clean stop.

## User flow worth showing
Landing-page / marketing site — no multi-screen app. Centerpiece is the real-work montage + the before/after transformation reveal (the site's one true interaction), plus the proof card. Rely on real photos and the site's own before/after mechanic rather than a fabricated app flow.

## Tone
- Preset: cinematic
- Creative direction: "gritty local-contractor trailer — real jobs, no fluff, built to last"
- Interpretation: big bold type, dramatic slow-push on real photos, hard green accents, restrained but confident pacing; one idea per scene; let the work breathe. Not chaotic, not corporate.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20s target

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #000000 (black) / #111111
- Accent: #1f8b2e (brand green)
- Text: #ffffff (on dark), #1c1c1c (on light)
- Display font: bold condensed sans (Anton or Archivo/Oswald feel) — matches the site's heavy tracking-tight headlines
- Body font: Inter / system sans
- Strongest visual element: real fence installation photos (gallery/fence-1..18.jpeg), the before/after slider (residential-2.png → fence-2.jpeg), AA logo (AA-Logo-02.png), green accent rail

## Share copy (draft)
30+ years of Rochester fence work, one new site. AA Fencing LLC is live — union contractor since 1992, real jobs in real yards. 🚧🟢 aafencing-llc.com

## Audio direction
- Role: cinematic support — low bed with a rising swell into the montage, hard hits on photo cuts and the "EST. 1992" close.
- Music: cinematic/driving bed with a clear pulse (bundled hyperframes track that fits a trailer feel). No music only if unavailable.
- Music treatment: start under the hook at low volume, swell as the logo settles, drive through the montage, small dip under the proof quote, final beat lands on the EST. 1992 card, fade out clean.
- Music cue guidance: track + tempo to be selected from bundled options at composition time. Target strong cues at (1) logo settle ~2.5s, (2) before/after divider sweep ~11s, (3) EST. 1992 close ~18s. Beat-grid the 4 montage labels onto every-other-beat with a full-set hold so each is readable.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle — let bass energy add a faint glow pulse to the green rail/accent, no waveform bars.
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched. A metallic/post-set "thunk" on each montage cut, a soft sweep on the before/after divider, one clean impact on the EST. 1992 lockup.
- Audio-coupled moments: montage label reveals (cut + thunk), before/after divider sweep (whoosh), credential stack (light ticks), EST. 1992 impact.
- Restraint rule: no cheesy stingers, no over-busy risers; this reads as a working contractor, not a mobile-game ad.

## Storyboard

Black. Green rail (#1f8b2e) draws left-to-right. Over a darkened real fence photo (fence-1 or hero-slider.png), "ROCHESTER'S TRUSTED FENCE COMPANY" slams in, "FENCE COMPANY" in green. Green pulse dot. AA logo settles bottom.
Sequential/interaction: yes — rail draws, then headline slams, then logo settles (3 beats).
Audio intent: low bed enters, first swell; a soft rail-draw sweep then a settle hit on the logo.
Audio-coupled idea: rail-draw whoosh + logo settle impact.
Music: cinematic bed, low, rising.
Transition mood: hard cut → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Real work montage — 6s
4 real gallery photos, ~1.4s each, slow Ken-Burns push. Each gets a short label bottom-left: VINYL PRIVACY → CHAIN LINK → ORNAMENTAL ALUMINUM → COMMERCIAL SECURITY. Green tick mark by each label.
Sequential/interaction: yes — photos cut one by one, label snaps in with each; beat-grid on every-other-beat, hold each label ~1.2s.
Audio intent: driving pulse, a metallic thunk on each cut.
Audio-coupled idea: photo cut + post-set thunk per image.
Music: bed drives.
Transition mood: hard cut → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Before / after transformation — 4s
Recreate the site's before/after slider as an automated reveal: residential-2.png (bare yard, BEFORE tag) with green divider sweeping right to reveal fence-2.jpeg (finished fence, AFTER tag). "SEE THE TRANSFORMATION" small caps top.

Audio intent: swell peaks as divider crosses; a soft sweep sound.
Audio-coupled idea: divider sweep whoosh.
Music: swell.
Transition mood: clean wipe → Scene 4

Audio-coupled idea: light tick per line reveal.

Audio-coupled idea: star-fill shimmer + count-up ticks.

Transition mood: hard cut to black → Scene 6

### Scene 6 — EST. 1992 close — 2.5s
Black. Green rail draws again. "EST. 1992" huge, "ROCHESTER, NY" under it. AA logo (AA-Logo-02.png), then "aafencing-llc.com" in green. Hard stop on final impact.

Audio intent: final hit lands on EST. 1992, tail fades clean.
Audio-coupled idea: one clean impact on the lockup.
Music: final beat + clean fade.
Transition mood: hard stop.

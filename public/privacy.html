Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 October 2026

AA Fencing LLC (“we”, “us”, “our”) operates this website. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and what choices you have.

Information we collect

This website has no contact form and collects nothing you type. The only ways to reach us from the site are telephone and email links, which open your own phone or mail app. What you send that way goes directly to us, not through this website.

  • Basic technical information. Like most websites, our host records standard server logs such as IP address, browser type and pages visited, for security and reliability.
  • Analytics. We do not use analytics or advertising trackers on this website.
  • Cookies. This website sets no cookies at all.

How we use what you send us

If you call or email us, we use your name and contact details to answer you, quote the work and carry out the job. We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not use it for automated decision making or profiling.

Fonts loaded from Google

Our page styling loads typefaces from Google Fonts. Your browser requests those font files directly from Google, which means Google receives your IP address and basic request information. Google does not set cookies for this and we receive nothing from it. A content blocker will stop that request and the site will still work with your device’s own fonts.

Data retention and security

We keep enquiry information only as long as we need it to answer you and for our reasonable business records, then delete it. Consistent with the New York SHIELD Act, we maintain reasonable safeguards to protect the personal information of New York residents. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information you have given us by emailing Anthony@scottslfc.com or calling (503) 560-5812. If you contacted us and no longer want to hear back, tell us and we will stop.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes
